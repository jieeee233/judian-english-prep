句点英语备课

内置示例 · A Quiet Afternoon

原文

P1 On my first day at a new school, I spent lunch looking for somewhere quiet to sit.

P2 When the rain began, I took shelter in the library, where a girl was reading by the window.

P3 She moved her books to make room for me. That small gesture made the unfamiliar school feel a little more like home.

时间状语从句与定语从句

When the rain began, I took shelter in the library, where a girl was reading by the window.

结构 When 引导时间状语从句。where 引导非限制性定语从句，补充说明图书馆里发生的事。

翻译 开始下雨时，我去图书馆避雨，那里有个女孩正坐在窗边看书。

推理判断

Why did the school begin to feel more like home?

查看答案与依据

女孩主动挪开书为主人公腾出位置。这个善意的举动让主人公开始感到被接纳。
